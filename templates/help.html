GET HELP

New to LandVerify?

Follow these simple steps to check a document.

01

Create an account

Select Sign in, then choose Create an account. Enter a username and password.

02

Save the trusted document

Sign in and select Add a record. Choose the original document you trust and save it.

03

Check a document

Select Check your document, upload the file, and select Check document. The record number is optional.

04

Read the answer

Green means REAL DOCUMENT. Red means FAKE DOCUMENT because it does not match the saved copy.

Something went wrong?

  • Choose the document again if the page asks you to refresh.
  • Use PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, or TXT files smaller than 10 MB.
  • If you do not have a saved original, the document may show as fake.
  • A matching file confirms it matches the saved copy; it does not by itself prove ownership or legal rights.
